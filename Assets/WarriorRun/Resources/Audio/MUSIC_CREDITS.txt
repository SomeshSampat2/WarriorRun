All tracks are "Free for Profit" type beats published by their
producers on YouTube for free use in monetised content, with
attribution. Normalised to -14 LUFS (EBU R128) and re-encoded
to 192 kbps MP3 for uniform loudness.

"Free for profit" beats permit monetised use as provided by each
