Warrior Run — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Warrior Run ("the game") is an offline endless-runner game developed by Somesh Sampat / Agentic Solutions ("we", "our"). This page explains how the game handles your information — in short: it doesn't collect any.

Data we collect

None. Warrior Run does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Specifically, the game has:

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No location, contacts, photos, or device-data access
  • No network requests — the game works fully offline

What stays on your device

Gameplay data such as high scores, coins, and settings is stored locally on your device only (via the operating system's app storage). It is never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app removes this data.

Third-party services

The game does not integrate any third-party service that receives your data. Google Play may collect its own usage/install statistics under Google's privacy policy, which is outside our control.

Children's privacy

Warrior Run does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes to collect data, this page will be updated and the effective date revised before that release ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via GitHub Issues.
